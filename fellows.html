GeoAI Fellows

A paid undergraduate fellowship funded by Responsible GeoAI for Civic Futures (Mozilla Foundation).

2–3student teams
$500per team
Spring 2027Demo Day

What Fellows do

Carry their final project forward into independent, public-facing work.

What is required

Fellows attend Demo Day and present their project there in Spring 2027.

How to apply

Applications open this semester, starting with students in GRG 356T, then more widely. Selection is competitive: pick a final project you would want to keep working on.

The application form and selection criteria will be posted here.
