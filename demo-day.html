Demo Day

Spring 2027. GeoAI Fellows present the projects they carried forward from the course.

Demo Day is where Fellows share their independent, public-facing work: maps, tools and analyses that began as GRG 356T final projects. Presenting in person is the one requirement of the fellowship; fall-round Fellows present at UT GIS Day instead.

Date, venue and programme will be announced here.
