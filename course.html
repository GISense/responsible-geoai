The course

GRG 356T Spatial Data Science and Maps, Department of Geography and the Environment, The University of Texas at Austin.

An undergraduate course in spatial data science and geospatial AI, taught by Dr. Yuhao Kang (GISense Lab). Each week pairs a lecture on concepts with a hands-on lab in Python, using open geocomputing tools: web data, spatial statistics, remote sensing, networks, street-view imagery and generative AI.

Responsible-computing questions are built into the labs rather than taught as a separate unit. Three of the labs are published as open teaching materials, free to reuse and adapt.
