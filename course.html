GRG 356T Spatial Data Science and Maps

Department of Geography and the Environment, The University of Texas at Austin. Fall 2026.

Instructor: Dr. Yuhao Kang, GISense Lab. Teaching assistant: Xihan Yao.

The course is cross-listed with the GeoAI Certificate, Behavioral Data Science, the School of Information, the Bridging Disciplines Programs, and Community and Regional Planning.

Format

Tuesdays, lecture: key concepts in spatial data science, GeoAI, spatial analysis and geovisualization. Thursdays, lab: hands-on sessions applying those concepts in Python with open geocomputing tools.

Students learn to

  • understand the concepts and principles of spatial data science, GeoAI and maps;
  • think spatially to address geographic and environmental challenges;
  • use geocomputing tools to solve real-world problems: spatial data scraping, spatial regression, clustering, remote sensing, network analysis, machine learning, geospatial AI and maps.
