About & licence

Responsible GeoAI for Civic Futures is led by Dr. Yuhao Kang at the GISense Lab, Department of Geography and the Environment, The University of Texas at Austin, with support from the Mozilla Foundation. The course materials were developed by Yuhao Kang, Xihan Yao and Brody Manquen.
